Resume Analyzer

See your resume the way screening software does.

Upload a PDF or Word file. You get an ATS compatibility score, a skills match against any job description, and specific notes on your wording.

  • ATS check shows what a parser can and can't read.
  • Job match lists the skills you have, and the ones you're missing.
  • Writing notes flag weak verbs and missing numbers.

Your file is sent to the analyzer service you're running and saved there. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Reading your resume…

Extracting text, finding sections, scoring ATS checks.

Skills listed after the word “Preferred” are scored as nice-to-haves. Everything before it counts as required.
